Basotho Traditional Dance and Music

Traditional music and dance are cornerstones of cultural heritage, offering a window into the history, beliefs, and values of communities around the world.
These art forms have been passed down through generations, often evolving while retaining core elements that reflect their origins.

instruments
  1. Lesiba-played by males and considered to be the national and most popular instrument
  2. Mamokhorong-combination of sekatari and setjoli
  3. Sekhankula-played by boys, especially shepherds
  4. Lekolulo-played by males
  5. Setolo-tolo-played by males
  6. Khabulu-bulu
  7. Lekhitla la lenaka
  8. Lekhitlane la lesapo-played by boys, especially shepherds
  9. Thomo-played by females
  10. Moropa-played by females
  11. Sekupu
  12. Sekebeku sa lehlaka-made using bamboo or maize stalks
  13. Sekebeku sa t'sepe
  14. Mokhope-made of hollowed river reed and a string of vegetable fibre
  15. Lekope-played by females
  16. Merutlhoana
  17. Lehofu
  18. Setjoli-played by males

dances
  1. Mokorotlo-perfomrmed by the chief and men on special occasions
  2. Mohobelo-performed by men, requires energy and endurance and is danced mainly in the evening.
  3. Mokhibo-women dance performed on knees
  4. Maqekha-is aspecial dance that forms part of the first rites of girls' initiation
